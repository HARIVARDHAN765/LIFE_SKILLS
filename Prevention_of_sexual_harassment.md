# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## 1.What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?
  
  * Making comments about someone's appearance in a sexual way.
  * Sharing inappropriate sexual content at work, school, or public places.
  * Asking for dates or personal relationships repeatedly after being refused.
  * Touching, hugging, or standing too close without permission.
  * Staring at someone's body in an uncomfortable way.
  * Sending unwanted sexual messages, pictures, or emails

## 2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

* First, I would tell the person to stop the behavior and make it clear that it is inappropriate
* f the behavior continues, I would warn them that I will report the incident to the concerned authorities.
* If it still does not stop, I would file a complaint through the proper channels.
* I will Report the incident to the appropriate authority, such as a manager, HR representative, teacher, or supervisor.
* I would support the affected person and encourage them to report the issue to the concerned authorities.
